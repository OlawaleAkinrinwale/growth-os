COMMON QUESTIONS

Clear answers.

If none of these address your question, ask it on the diagnostic call.

Q/01 So are you against raising money?

No. I'm against using funding as a substitute for solving fundamental growth problems. Capital can be an extraordinary accelerator once the underlying economics make sense.

Q/02 What kind of businesses do you work with?

My focus is founder-led ecommerce businesses with proven demand and enough existing activity to diagnose meaningful problems across acquisition, conversion and retention.

Q/03 Do you only work on one channel?

No. The Growth OS is built around business constraints rather than marketing channels. If retention is the bottleneck, buying more traffic isn't the answer. If conversion is already strong but qualified demand is weak, rebuilding email flows isn't the answer either. We work on the constraint.

Q/04 What happens on the first call?

We spend approximately 30 minutes understanding the business and determining where the most likely constraint sits. If I believe I can help, I'll explain what I would investigate next. If I don't, I'll tell you.

Q/05 Will you guarantee results?

No serious operator can guarantee a specific growth outcome before understanding the business. What I can guarantee is that the work begins with a baseline, a clear hypothesis and measurable criteria for determining whether the intervention worked.
